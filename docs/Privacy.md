# Privacy policy — Adventure Mapper

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Adventure Mapper is a local map editor. It does not collect, transmit or sell
personal data.

## What the app stores

* **Maps you create or open** — saved as `.ama` files in the location you choose,
  and as autosave drafts in the app's own Documents folder.
* **Preferences** — the autosave interval and the UI language, stored by the
  operating system's preferences store on the device.

These stay on the device. The app has no account system, no analytics, no
advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and makes no network requests.

## Files you hand to other apps

Export and print send a document to the operating system's file saver or print
service at your request. Those copies are then governed by the app or service
you chose.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Data deletion

Delete the map files, the app's Documents folder, or the app itself. Removing the
app removes its preferences and its drafts.
